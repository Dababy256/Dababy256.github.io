About

Dr. Apule Gerald Humble is a Ugandan medical doctor and graduate of Makerere University College of Health Sciences. His clinical experience spans general medical practice, emergency care, and maternal and child healthcare, including work in resource-limited settings.

He is interested in improving access to quality healthcare, strengthening referral systems and supporting evidence-informed health service delivery.
